# WotLK Achievements Applicable to Vanilla (1.12) Content, Sorted by Bartle Type

> 2026-09-28: the Adventure Journal sorts its challenges by Quantic Foundry's six pairs instead (the user chose Option A; see
> player-motivation-models.md). Mapping for this list: Achiever's General / Professions / Reputation to Achievement (Completion,
> Power) or Social (Community) for reputations; Dungeons & Raids and Feats of Strength to Mastery (Challenge, Strategy); Quests to
> Immersion (Story); Explorer to Creativity (Discovery); Socializer's world events to Social (Community) or Immersion (Fantasy);
> Killer to Social (Competition), and the chaotic ones (mass kills, falls, Leeroy) to Action. In game so far (0.97.0): Got My Mind
> On My Money (100 / 1,000 / 5,000 gold), Honorable Kills (1 / 100 / 1,000 / 5,000), Exalted (1 / 5 / 10), Quests Completed
> (50 / 250 / 1,000), Pest Control (10 / 50 / 100 critters), and ours: Epic Finds, Beasts Tamed, Wanderer, On the Wind, Good
> Company, Thinning the Wilds.

## 1. System origin

The Achievement system was added in **patch 3.0.2 (October 14, 2008)**, the *Wrath of the Lich King* pre-patch — "a new button was added...in the micro menu at the center of the main action bar." It organized achievements into categories including **General, Quests, Exploration, Player vs. Player, Dungeons & Raids, Professions, Reputation, World Events**, and the points-free **Feats of Strength**. This document uses the WotLK-era (3.3.5) achievement list, pulled live from Wowhead's WotLK-flavor database, and keeps only entries whose *content* existed in patch 1.12 (levels 1-60, Eastern Kingdoms/Kalimdor, vanilla dungeons/raids, vanilla battlegrounds, vanilla professions and world events).

Format per line: **Name | original requirement | proposed vanilla-server requirement (if reworked) | notes**

## 3. EXPLORER (discovery, zones, lore)

### Exploration
All are "Explore [zone], revealing the covered areas of the world map," 10 points each unless noted. These map straight onto vanilla content with no rework needed — only the zone list changes (drop every Outland/Northrend/Cataclysm-added zone).

**Eastern Kingdoms zones:** Elwynn Forest, Westfall, Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Deadwind Pass, Loch Modan, Dun Morogh, Wetlands, The Hinterlands, Arathi Highlands, Badlands, Searing Gorge, Burning Steppes, Swamp of Sorrows, Blasted Lands, Tirisfal Glades, Silverpine Forest, Hillsbrad Foothills, Western Plaguelands, Eastern Plaguelands, Stranglethorn Vale.

**Kalimdor zones:** Durotar, The Barrens, Mulgore, Teldrassil, Darkshore, Ashenvale, Stonetalon Mountains, Thousand Needles, Desolace, Feralas, Dustwallow Marsh, Azshara, Tanaris, Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Winterspring, Silithus, Moonglade.

## 6. Other exclusions worth flagging

- **Hunter/pet-specific achievements**: none exist in the original WotLK achievement list — pet-family "Hunting Ground" style achievements weren't added until Cataclysm, so there's nothing from the WotLK-era system to adapt here.
- **Dual Talent Specialization, Shave and a Haircut (Barber Shop), Ring of the Kirin Tor, Higher Learning, the Dalaran mammoth/bear pets, "Get to the Choppa!," "My Storage is Gigantique"**: all require Northrend/Dalaran-specific NPCs, systems or items that don't exist in 1.12.
- **Arena, Wintergrasp, Isle of Conquest, Eye of the Storm, all Gladiator/Duelist/Rival/Challenger titles**: arena launched in TBC; those three battlegrounds are TBC/Wrath content.
- **All "Realm First!" achievements** (level 80 by race, Grand Master professions at skill 450, raid-boss race-to-server-first): don't fit a solo/low-population server conceptually (no competing population) even before considering the content-tier mismatch.
- **Master/Grand Master profession and First Aid tiers**: require the 375/450 skill caps added in TBC/Wrath; vanilla caps out at 300 (Artisan).
- **Riding tiers beyond the vanilla two** (Journeyman/Expert flying, etc.): flying mounts don't exist pre-TBC.
