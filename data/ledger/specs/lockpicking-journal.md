# The Lockpicking journal: the rogues' tab

Asked 2026-10-08 (session 69, the user): "Lockpicking we never really built a tab for. We need that too for rogues", then "lockpicking
tab / journal. Do research on all the locked boxes, their levels, locations etc. Where to practice lockpicking and other things
related to that." The research and the database page came first (tools/server-data/lockpicking.py, the Companion's
lockpicking.html); this is the in-game tab, to be previewed on the web before it goes in.

## What it is

The gathering journals' frame (docs/specs/gathering-journals.md, GatherJournal.lua) for a fourth skill, shown to rogues only:

- **A side tab** "Lockpicking Journal" in the professions window, after the secondary skills, for a rogue who has learned Pick
  Lock. Lockpicking has no window of its own and no recipes, like Herbalism and Skinning.
- **The rank bar** at the top: Lockpicking's value and its cap (5 × level), and the next step ("Level 34 lets you reach 170.").
- **The list** (search, Filter, folding categories):
  - The journal ("The Lockpicker's Journal", "Open the Journal").
  - **Where to Practice**, by skill band (1–25 … 276–300): the band's locks that still teach, folded but for yours.
  - **Lockboxes** by how they come: From Pockets (the junkboxes), Dropped and Found, Practice and Quests.
  - **Chests of the World** by zone, **Locks in the Dungeons** by dungeon (doors and chests).
  - **Keys and Charges**: what opens a lock for anyone (the blacksmiths' skeleton keys, the engineers' Seaforium charges).
  - A lock you've opened shows [n]; one above your skill is dimmed.
- **The card** (the profession card art; a rogue's own, if Forever has one, else the gathering cards' frame):
  - Journal: "37 locks opened since you first worked a pick into one." Your most opened, your favorite places, what you've found inside
    (the best items, the money).
  - A band: what teaches there, each lock in your skill's colour.
  - A lockbox: its icon and tooltip, its four steps (orange, yellow, green, gray), where it comes from (the pockets, levels and
    best chances; the creatures that drop it), what's inside (the best items, the money), "You opened N."
  - A chest or door: its zone or dungeon, how many stand there, its steps, "You opened N."
  - A tool: who makes it, what it opens.
- **Colours** (Player::UpdateGatherSkill, Lockpicking as Herbalism): orange to the lock's skill + 25, yellow to + 50, green to
  + 100, gray after; red above your skill. A lock of skill 1 counts as 5.
- **Filter:** For my skill, Opened, Not opened yet, Collapse all, Expand all, Clear filters.
- **Counting:** per character, `ForeverUIStats.gathering.lock` = `{ since, total, items, zones, opened = { [lock] = n }, money }`:
  a Pick Lock cast's target (an item in the bags, an object in the world) when the spell succeeds (UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED for
  the player with Pick Lock), its loot when it is taken.
- **No secure part:** as the other journals.

## The data

`tools/server-data/lockpicking.py` writes the site's JSON; the addon's data (a `LockpickingData` beside `GatheringData`) comes from
the same reads: the boxes (id, skill, sources in short, best contents, money), the chests and doors (name, kind, skill, zones
and counts), the tools, the bands. Lock skill from Lock.dbc (lock type 1); the four steps from UpdateGatherSkill.

## Built in game (Forever UI 0.260.0, session 70)

The user, session 69: "looks good to build, I will refine in game". Built from the approved preview, its defaults kept: rogues
only; the blacksmiths' card art; the Practice Lockboxes in the 1–25 band and pointed to under 75; "For my skill" off by default.

- **Files:** `addons/ForeverUI/LockpickJournal.lua` (the list, the cards, the counting) and `LockpickingData.lua` (generated by
  `tools/server-data/lockpicking_lua.py` from lockpicking.py's JSON: one line per lockbox, chest or door and key; names, ids and
  numbers only). Both load after GatherJournal.lua (a new file: the client restarted).
- **The frame is GatherJournal.lua's:** `ns.RegisterGatherJournal("lock", def)` gives a journal its own skill, counts, list,
  Filter menu and card on the same page (rank bar, list, card, search, folds). The gathering journals got the hooks for it: a
  row's right-hand number in a colour (the lock's skill), a row's own colour and dim, a header's own fold rule, a slot that
  selects something other than an item, the item head on the card (`kit.head`), a tooltip on the rank bar.
- **The tab:** Professions.lua makes the page on every book (our window's and the skinned game windows') and a tab in every tab
  set; `layoutTabs` shows it last, after Poisons, only when `ns.LockpickKnown` says so (class ROGUE and Pick Lock in the spell
  book). The skill is read from the Lockpicking skill line (the last value seen kept in the counts while its header is folded).
- **Folds:** the bands fold but for yours (a band you open or close stays so); Expand all opens every band, Collapse all folds
  the five categories. `ns.db.gatherFold.lock`, `ns.db.lockContext` (For my skill).
- **Counting:** `ForeverUIStats.gathering.lock` as above, plus `skill`. UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT names the target (a lockbox by its
  name, else a chest or door, the one standing in your zone when several share a name); when the cast names none, the bag item
  clicked in the 15 s before (PickupContainerItem / UseContainerItem, hooked) stands in. A chest's loot within 10 s of its pick
  is counted as you take it; a lockbox's when it is opened from the bags (a right-click: UseContainerItem). Coins by
  PLAYER_MONEY while the lock's loot window is open (and a second after).
- **Differences from the preview:** Practice Lock is said to be made by engineers (the preview said blacksmiths; it is an
  Engineering recipe). A dungeon's place goes under Locks in the Dungeons and a world place under Chests of the World per
  place (the Workshop Door under Dun Morogh and Gnomeregan), not by the lock. The chests and doors on a band's card are lines, not
  links (the list has them). Names are the client's own (GetItemInfo), the data's until the client has the item.
- **Tests:** `addons/tests/foreverui/115-lockpicking-journal.lua` (the data, the steps and colours, the tab for a rogue with
  Pick Lock only, the layout, the bands and categories, both sides' zones, For my skill, the counting from casts and loot, the
  cards, the Filter, the search, a fight); the class matrix's "lockpicking tab" step (every class given Pick Lock: the tab a
  rogue's only). The stub learned SpellIsTargeting (a spell on a bag item leaves it there) and UseContainerItem away from a
  merchant (used, not sold).

To check in game: what UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT gives as the target for a lockbox in the bags (the name, or nothing: the fallback
then); whether a chest's loot window opens on its own after Pick Lock (if not, its contents aren't counted, only the opening).

## Built in game: the places to practice and the map pin (0.261.0, session 70)

The user, after the research (docs/research/forever-lockpicking-quests.md) and the preview's update: "Go ahead with the updates to
the lockpicking". Built from the preview (dbsite/build/win_lockpick.py); the greyed lock-pass entries are gone, not greyed.

- **The lock pass** (`tools/server-data/lockpicking.py`'s `REMOVED`, each with its reason): Venture Co. Strongbox (its ledger is
  wanted by no quest here), Tazan's Satchel (a level-10 quest bag Tazan's Key opens), Lucius's Lockbox the item and Locked Gift
  (nothing on the server gives them). The chest named Lucius's Lockbox at Alther's Mill stays. 19 lockboxes, 50 chests and doors.
  Lockboxes' Practice and Quests group is empty now and doesn't show.
- **Quest chests** (`NOTES`): Lucius's Lockbox, Ambermill Strongbox, Duskwood Chest, Gallywix's Lockbox (each "On a quest: “…”, for
  … rogues. It opens only while you’re on it."), Cozzle's Footlocker ("Goblin Sponsorship", any class; Cozzle's Key opens it too),
  Benedict's Chest (its key). The line is on the chest's card.
- **The places** (`PLACES`, exported as `L.places`: 1.14.2's uiMapID and x, y 0-1; Forever's Redridge map is shifted, its numbers
  are not used): Alther's Mill (Redridge 52.0, 45.3; the Alliance's quest, three steps: the trainer, Renzik in Old Town, Lucius in
  Lakeshire), the Tide Razor (the Barrens 65.1, 45.4; the Horde's: the trainer, Shenthul and Zando'zan in the Cleft of Shadow,
  Wrenix in Ratchet), and 13 footlocker spots from our spawns (Lake Everstill to Hearthglen), labelled as the journal's own picks.
  A place is under every band where one of its locks is orange or yellow.
- **The list:** each band's places first (after About), in gold, the quest's side at the right in its colour; your side's quest
  place first, then the other side's, then the spawns'. Search finds them by name or zone; Opened / Not opened yet hide them; For
  my skill keeps those with a lock that fits.
- **A place's card:** where and whose quest (or both factions), what the quest asks in our words, the zone's map (340 x 227: the
  zone's picture from `ns.ZoneArtPieces`, the HUD zone map's own pieces, now shared; the spot's waypoint mark, lit while it's your
  pin; a caption with the coordinates and "pinned"), **Pin on Map** / **Remove Pin**, what to pick there (skill, how many here),
  the quest chest's line, and the steps, each with a small pin.
- **The pin:** `ns.SetMapPin(map, x, y)` (MapPins.lua: one pin per character; a second place's pin replaces the first, so the
  first's button turns back to Pin on Map); Remove Pin is `ns.ClearMapPin`. A toast in the loot stack confirms it: the item toast
  (as Nodes Revealed), "Map Pin Placed" in white or "Map Pin Removed" in gray, the place's name and its zone and coordinates, the
  map icon; no chime (the pin plays Forever's waypoint sound).
- **The band card:** "Places to practice:" with a pin on each line. **The chest card:** "Practice there:" with the place's pin.
  **The journal under 75:** your side's place named ("Lucius in Lakeshire sends you…", "Wrenix the Wretched in Ratchet sends
  you…") with Pin on Map.
- **The frame:** GatherJournal.lua's kit grew `button` (a red button in the card's flow), `pinLine` (a line with a small pin at
  its right) and `map` (the zone's map with a spot), pooled per card. No new Lua file (a /reload is enough).
- **Differences from the preview:** the places on a band's or chest's card are lines with their pins, not links (the list has
  them). The meta words of the preview ("Forever's trainers…", "as 1.12's do") are left out of the game: "Your rogue trainer
  starts you on the way at level 16" and "the journal's own pick".
- **Tests:** 115-lockpicking-journal.lua: the removals and notes, the places per faction (Alliance and Horde order), the list rows,
  the place cards (both sides', a spawn spot), the pin button calling `ns.SetMapPin(1433, 0.52, 0.453)` and turning to Remove
  Pin and back, a step's pin (1453), the band card's pins, the journal's pointer for a Horde rogue, the two toasts (quiet).
