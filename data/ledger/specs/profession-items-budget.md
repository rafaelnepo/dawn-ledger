# Profession items against the item budget

Written by `python3 tools/server-data/item_budget.py report` (session 69). The rule and its numbers: [docs/server/item-budget.md](../server/item-budget.md). 100 % is on budget; weapons are judged by their stats only (their damage per second is beside them). "Use:" and chance-on-hit effects are priced flat (about 12 points), so an item built around one reads as over or under: those are marked. Below item level 20 one stat point is a big share: read those loosely.
