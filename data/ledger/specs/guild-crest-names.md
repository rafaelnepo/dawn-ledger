# Guild Crest names

Names for Forever UI’s Guild Crest window. The game numbers its tabard emblems (170) and border styles (10) but names none of them, so these are our own. Data: [guild-crest-names.json](guild-crest-names.json), keyed by the game’s index numbers as in the Guild Crest mockup’s `crest.json`.

## How they were named

Each emblem was composed flat from its two tabard textures (upper and lower chest, each rolled by half its width so the front sits in the middle), in colour 14 (near white), and each border in colour 00; the tiles were read off contact sheets (a scratch script, not in the repo; the art stays in `(a file on the server's Mac)`).

- One to three words, title case, what the picture shows; heraldic words (rampant, combatant, displayed, saltire, chevron) only where they read clearly.
- American spelling; curly apostrophes, as the style guide asks.
- No two emblems share a name: look-alikes get a plain distinguishing word (Wide-Eyed Cat / Smiling Cat, Mushroom / Toadstool).
- Nothing tied to a faction: both sides use every emblem.
- The flattened art is small and split at the seam; names marked (?) below are best guesses: 2, 26, 41, 51, 52, 61, 66, 69, 80, 91, 96, 102, 108, 112, 129, 131, 136, 139, 159, 160, 161, 162.
